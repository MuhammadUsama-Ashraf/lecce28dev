Fumo di Cocco: the scent, note by note

Fumo di Cocco is the unisex signature that runs through the Lecce 28 body collection — the body wash, the lotion, the butter and the scrub all share it, so a full ritual layers rather than competes.

Top notes: Italian Bergamot, Guatemala Cardamom, Incense. Middle notes: White Tuberose, Sea Coconut, Clove. Base notes: White Musk, Tonka Bean, Amber, Smoked Guaiac.

Prefer your skincare quiet? The Natural Body Butter is also offered entirely unscented, with the same over-98% natural formula.

Questions about a formula? Email support@lecce28.com.
